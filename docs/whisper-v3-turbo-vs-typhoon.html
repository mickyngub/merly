Short verdict

Typhoon is cleaner on the Windows/PowerShell part, but it is not strictly better across the full sample yet because it dropped the first ~30 seconds as low-confidence junk.

Where Turbo wins

Coverage. Turbo produced text for nearly the whole 51s clip. Typhoon only kept the final ~20.6s under the current settings.

Practical Takeaway

For this exact sample, Typhoon is not a blanket “better by X%” replacement yet. The honest read is: Typhoon is better quality when it locks onto the Thai speech, but Whisper large-v3-turbo is better at covering the whole clip under these settings.

If the goal is a creator workflow, the next best step is to test on a full clip and tune Typhoon’s segmentation so it stops dropping the first half. If that still fails, use turbo for coverage and Typhoon for higher-quality retries on the bad code-switching sections, or test full non-turbo large-v3 / Pathumma.
